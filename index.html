What is Level Up?

A half-day professional development event built by young Toowoomba professionals, for young Toowoomba professionals. Less lanyard-and-lecture, more skills you use on Monday.

Skills, not slogans

Short, sharp sessions on the things nobody teaches you: intergenerational relationships, health and wellness, and leadership.

Access, not autographs

Meet the Boss is a fireside chat. Four local leaders answering the questions the room actually wants answered — a real conversation, not a handshake and a photo.

Built here, on purpose

You shouldn't have to drive to Brisbane to grow. This is regional ambition, hosted at home.

Questions

1-1 Do I have to be a chamber member? +
No. Level Up is open to anyone building a career or a business in the Toowoomba region.
1-2 How long is it? Do I need the whole day off? +
It's a half day — roughly four hours door to door, including the breaks. Exact start time is confirmed with the venue.
1-3 What actually happens at Meet the Boss? +
It's a fireside chat. Four local leaders take the stage and answer questions — we open with ones gathered beforehand, so the awkward but useful ones actually get asked, then it's over to the floor. Bring the question you'd never get to ask over email.
1-4 Is there an age limit? +
It's aimed at 18 to 35 year olds, but nobody's checking your licence at the door — open to anyone who's interested.
1-5 What will tickets cost? +
Pricing lands with the venue announcement. Our brief to ourselves: cheap enough that a first-year employee can say yes without asking their boss.
1-6 I hate networking. Should I still come? +
Especially then. Everything is seated and structured — talks, a panel, then Meet the Boss. You're never standing in the middle of a room holding a drink wondering who to talk to.
