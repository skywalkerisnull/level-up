What is Level Up?

A professional development night built by young Toowoomba professionals, for young Toowoomba professionals. Less lanyard-and-lecture, more skills you use on Monday.

Skills, not slogans

Short, sharp sessions on the things nobody teaches you — negotiating, pricing yourself, leading before you have the title.

A room worth being in

Founders, tradies, public servants, creatives. Structured intros so you leave with names, not a pocket of cards.

Built here, on purpose

You shouldn't have to drive to Brisbane to grow. This is regional ambition, hosted at home.

1-5

Questions

1-1 Do I have to be a chamber member? +
No. Level Up is open to anyone building a career or a business in the Toowoomba region. Members just get first dibs on seats.
1-2 Is there an age limit? +
It's aimed at 20 to 40 year olds, but nobody's checking your licence at the door. Come if the content is for you.
1-3 What will tickets cost? +
Pricing lands with the venue announcement. Our brief to ourselves: cheap enough that a first-year employee can say yes without asking their boss.
1-4 I hate networking. Should I still come? +
Especially then. The night is structured so you're never standing in the middle of a room holding a drink wondering who to talk to.
